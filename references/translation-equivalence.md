# Translation Equivalence Review

Use this reference whenever English source items and Chinese translated/adapted items are both present. Fluency is not enough; the review must test whether the Chinese item preserves the measurement meaning of the English item.

## Core rule

For each English-Chinese item pair, write down the English item's core meaning before judging the Chinese. Then compare the Chinese item against that core meaning.

Flag the item when the Chinese:

- Adds concepts absent from the English item.
- Omits key concepts from the English item.
- Changes the actor, object, referent, or observation target.
- Changes intensity, frequency, certainty, or evaluative tone.
- Changes ability into actual behavior, learning into skilled use, inspiration into enlightenment, advocacy into pressure, or knowledge into expertise.
- Converts an item into a causal claim, performance outcome, attitude, or moral judgment that was not in the original.
- Makes the item sound more socially desirable, accusatory, or normative than the source item.

## Minimum row-by-row checks

| Check | Question | Example risk |
|---|---|---|
| Added content | Does the Chinese include a word or idea not in the English? | English says "benefits"; Chinese adds "AI improves performance" |
| Missing content | Does the Chinese drop an essential object or qualifier? | "appropriately learn AI technology" becomes only "use AI" |
| Meaning shift | Did the psychological meaning change? | "learn" becomes "skilled use"; "inspires me" becomes "deeply enlightened" |
| Actor shift | Did the actor change? | Top managers -> direct supervisor |
| Referent shift | Did the target group change? | managers -> us; group members -> team members |
| Level shift | Did item wording move from individual to collective or unit level? | "convince me" vs "convince us" |
| Valence shift | Did positive/negative direction change? | fit -> misfit, without explicit reverse-scoring logic |
| Observability | Can the respondent reasonably know this? | Employee rates leader's actual expertise instead of perceived knowledge |
| Response fit | Does the response scale match the wording? | Frequency item with agreement anchors |

## Severity guide

- P1: Large meaning shift, added/missing construct content, actor/referent/level shift that changes the construct, or translation that would fail a back-translation review.
- P2: Moderate wording drift, intensity change, awkward but interpretable expression, or referent ambiguity that can be fixed in wording.
- P3: Minor fluency, punctuation, or idiomatic polishing that does not affect measurement meaning.

## Chinese reporting language

Use concrete evidence. Avoid saying only "翻译不准确".

Preferred pattern:

`英文核心含义是……；当前中文改成了……，新增/遗漏/改变了……。这会影响……。建议改为……。`

Examples:

- `appropriately learn artificial intelligence technology` is closer to "恰当地学习/掌握 AI 技术", not simply "熟练使用 AI 技术".
- `My job inspires me` is closer to "我的工作激励/鼓舞我", not "使我深受启发" if the latter sounds like gaining intellectual insight rather than motivational inspiration.
- `I try to convince my group members...` may become `我试图说服团队成员...`; if changed to `我的领导会努力让我们...`, explicitly flag the referent and level-of-analysis change.
